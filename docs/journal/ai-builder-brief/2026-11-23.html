TOP 10 · November 23, 2026 #1 Hugging Face Daily Paper (80 upvotes; `arXiv:2611.27193`, code released)

cmHC stabilizes 300B-MoE hyper-connections via Birkhoff contraction and cuts Ascend latency 44.8%

Researchers from China Telecom's Xingchen AGI Lab, Huawei Technologies, HKUST(GZ), and HIT(SZ) diagnosed why DeepSeek-style Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (`mHC`) stall or collapse: residual mixing matrices near Birkhoff polytope vertices under-mix into isolated streams, while matrices clustered near the uniform barycenter erase stream diversity. Their zero-extra-parameter fix, `cmHC`, geometrically contracts the Birkhoff polytope toward its interior (`λ = 0.97` at 6B, `λ = 0.96` at 300B) with gentler contraction in shallow layers to preserve gradient flow.
