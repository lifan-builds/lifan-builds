NVIDIA PAIR turns spare PCs into a local inference pool for multi-agent work

NVIDIA launched the open-source Personal AI Router beta, which discovers machines on a local network and routes independent Ollama or LM Studio requests to an eligible node without changing the agent harness. It supports recent RTX hardware, DGX Spark, and Apple M4+ systems; requests stay on one node rather than splitting a single inference across GPUs.

WeatherNext 3 brings hourly, 5km AI forecasts into Google products and Cloud

Google DeepMind and Google Research launched WeatherNext 3, which combines live geostationary-satellite mosaics with conventional analysis and refreshes forecasts hourly. It produces key surface variables at 5km resolution, other surface variables at 10km, and atmospheric variables at 25km—roughly five times sharper than WeatherNext 2—while adding precipitation and clean-energy variables.
