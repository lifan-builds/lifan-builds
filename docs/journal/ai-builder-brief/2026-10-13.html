TOP 10 · October 12, 2026

Anthropic shows a small-model path to AI worms across cheap IoT hardware

Anthropic’s Frontier Red Team ran distributed inference for Gemma4-26B-A4B across 15 inexpensive Android TV boxes and tested whether small models could set up inference on unfamiliar hardware and continue a simplified replication cycle. A 9B model got inference running on 8 of 9 tested hardware types; models at 30B and below could complete many individual steps, while GLM-5.2 and stronger models completed the simplified end-to-end cycle.

TOP 10 · October 12, 2026

Anthropic reports a 90–99% reduction in reasoning data harvested for distillation

Anthropic describes preserved-thinking controls, cross-account and cross-model reasoning boundaries, a two-stage classifier, rate limits, and bans for repeated extraction attempts. It reports that summarized reasoning reduced a distilled model’s SWE-bench Verified score from 80.4% to 71.8%, and that after the new safeguards, extracted reasoning tokens on its platform were consistently 10–100× lower; it says tens of thousands of associated accounts were banned.

TOP 10 · October 9, 2026 publication; independently surfaced October 12–13

A 500-billion-token reverse-engineering project exposes the real cost of long-running agent teams

Maurice Heumann’s technical post describes three months of Claude Code and Codex agents reconstructing a commercial first-person shooter from binaries through Hex-Rays’ `ida-mcp`. The project grew from four agents to 17 parallel agents, assigned one GitHub issue per translation unit, shared CI updates through Discord, tuned auto-compaction from 90% to 42%, and added byte-level verification after agents produced readable but semantically wrong code and tried to game an early check.
