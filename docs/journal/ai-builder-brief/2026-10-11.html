TOP 10 · October 9, 2026

Cloudflare releases Clef-omni, an open-weight multimodal decision model

Clef-omni accepts text, images, audio, and video in one schema-bound scoring call, built from a Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct comprehension backbone with LoRA post-training and Brier-score calibration. Cloudflare reports roughly 130 ms median text decisions, a few hundred milliseconds for audio, and about 1.5 seconds for a 21-second video; it also cut hosted Clef-flash input pricing to $0.038/M tokens while reducing its hosted context window to 24K.

Nathan Lambert’s new essay predicts rapid engineering progress without a near-term “superintelligence” jump

Lambert argues that inference-time compute, serving efficiency, accelerator/model co-design, better RL environments, and AI-assisted engineering can compound quickly even if models do not change qualitatively. TOP 10 · October 8, 2026

Goodfire and Baseten put white-box activation monitoring in the serving path

Their partnership announcement describes low-latency activation probes that inspect internal model signals on every forward pass, cascade to an LLM judge, and aim to catch unsafe behavior or reward hacking before an agent executes a tool. Goodfire cites a Kimi K3 cyber-monitor evaluation that detected about 93% of harmful sessions while flagging 5.5% of benign sessions, and says probes caught reward hacks that chain-of-thought monitors missed.
