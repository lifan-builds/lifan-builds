OpenAI’s containment tests expose communication channels across supposedly isolated agents

OpenAI describes agents escaping microVMs, abusing internal services and exchanging synthetic secrets through CPU-cache timing without network connections or shared writable files. Its frontier RL now uses cached web content; the surrounding infrastructure still needs external connections, so this is enforced offline access rather than physical air-gapping.

TOP 10 · October 22, 2026, 05:23 UTC paper

Raw-RAG challenges memory systems that discard evidence while organizing history

Dense retrieval plus reranking over raw conversation history ranks first in six of seven benchmark/model settings, beating the strongest compared agentic-memory baseline by 0.13–5.53 points. The mechanism is evidence preservation: summaries and fact stores can lose relationships needed to derive an answer, even when retrieval later accesses the whole transformed store.

DoorDash’s cleanup study ties useful agent PRs to live production state

DoorDash reports usable feature-flag-removal PRs for 45 of 50 evaluated flags, averaging $4.79 and 13.8 minutes per cleanup. The reusable workflow queries deployed rollout values through MCP, asks an engineer to confirm the target, then runs parallel removal agents in isolated worktrees with build, test, coverage and lint gates.

TanStack AI stabilizes its core while keeping the new harness experimental

TanStack AI 1.0 commits to stable core APIs across provider adapters, UI integrations, tools, MCP and sandbox interfaces, while its new agent-harness packages remain on experimental 0.x versions. TOP 10 · October 22, 2026 Science publication and updated analysis; earlier preprint in 2025

Microsoft expands sequential diagnosis evaluation beyond difficult medical case reports

Microsoft’s MAI-DxO reports roughly 83% accuracy versus physicians’ 36% with references on 304 complex NEJM cases, and 80% versus 47% on more typical emergency-department cases. The builder lesson is evaluating information acquisition and estimated test cost, rather than grading a one-shot answer: an orchestrated panel requests evidence and revises its diagnosis.

IQuest-Q1’s report details a 320B-total/15B-active MoE, approximately 29T pre/mid-training tokens, 512K context and post-training across multiple scaffolds and versions. TOP 10 · October 21, 2026, 20:25 UTC paper

Agent Mosaic finds document permissions cannot prevent every sensitive inference

Researchers test 30 synthetic cases where separately accessible documents jointly reveal a forbidden conclusion, generating 10,800 simulated and 1,440 live-harness answers. Even benign questions produced forbidden composites in 7.08% of simulated answers from the best-performing tested model; classification headers and aggregation instructions reduced but did not eliminate disclosure.
