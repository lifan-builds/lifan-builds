Digitaliko explains why agent review gates must detect missing reviewers

Filip Papranec describes an AI review job that stayed green after an action upgrade removed its prompt, alongside judges that chased new issues indefinitely or accepted tests that only searched source text. His internal design runs deterministic checks first, uses a fresh read-only reviewer with typed findings, treats absent verdicts as failure and bounds repeat review.
