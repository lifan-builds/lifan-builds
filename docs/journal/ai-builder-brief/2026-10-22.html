OpenAI requires gateway approval and restricts competitor-training inputs from November

Updated policies require prior review for services giving third parties direct model access, including routers and gateways; merely embedding a model inside an application does not trigger that requirement. Sierra’s multilingual benchmark separates successful tasks from usable voice conversations

Six-language evaluation holds service tasks fixed while localizing identities, voices and conventions; across 2,700 calls, Korean task completion trails English by 22.6 percentage points on average, and the most responsive agent is not the most natural speaker. The accompanying entity analysis highlights exact capture and repair, extending the current leaderboard across six languages; its underlying elicitation paper is September research, not a new October discovery.

Dharma’s tokenizer experiment cuts serving cost while preserving decision tokens

TOP 10 · October 21, 2026 experiment and retrospective

WorldTUI’s tiny terminal model shows why fluent output can hide lost memory

Simon Edwardsson trained a terminal-screen simulator using 336,000 recorded episodes, screen deltas and correction examples, then pruned its vocabulary to 333 tokens for a 360M-parameter, 208 MB browser demo. The sharp lesson comes from ablation: removing one layer barely changed next-token accuracy but collapsed exact readbacks from 64/64 to zero in that checkpoint.
