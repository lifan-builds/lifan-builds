Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and gates Mythos 5.1

Anthropic released Fable 5.1 broadly and made the more sensitive Mythos 5.1 available to trusted partners, bundling improved coding, research, computer use and long-running problem solving with a 75% cut in prompt-cache read pricing, to $0.25 per million tokens. Input/output list prices remain $10/$50 per million tokens; Anthropic estimates typical workloads cost about 25% less and highly agentic ones up to 45% less.

World Labs unveils Atlas, a unified generative/reconstructive 3D world model

Fei-Fei Li's World Labs introduced Atlas, a multimodal world model that can generate image/video frames with precise camera control and reconstruct scenes into navigable 3D representations from a small number of images. Why it matters: Atlas combines generation and reconstruction in one system, pointing toward reusable world state rather than isolated video clips.

Meta releases Muse Voice Transcribe for real-time multilingual speech

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Voice Transcribe, its first real-time audio-perception model, combining streaming ASR, speaker diarization and endpoint detection across more than 70 languages. Why it matters: one low-latency model handling transcription, turn boundaries and speakers can simplify voice-agent stacks and reduce orchestration latency.
