OpenAI says it has reached the “automated research intern” milestone

OpenAI says its coding agents can now complete well-defined research tasks that would take a skilled researcher several days, meeting the “automated research intern” target it set last fall. Its internal telemetry is the more important evidence: by mid-August, the median researcher was using more than $600 of inference per day at API prices; the 90th percentile exceeded $7,000; and the research organization consumed 3.1 agent-workdays for each human workday.

In OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki says no lab has solved alignment and monitoring well enough to keep scaling at maximum speed “for much longer,” expects voluntary slowdowns to become commonplace, and argues that Preparedness Framework-style thresholds should become widely mandated and independently audited. Mostik replaces text handoffs with a learned bridge between model hidden states

Four-month-old startup Mostik emerged from stealth with a system that passes a large model’s latent state through a separately trained bridge into a smaller model, avoiding text as the inter-model bottleneck while keeping both models frozen. In its disclosed GLM-5.2 753B-to-Qwen-3.5 4B experiment, the large model performs only prefill and never decodes; the small model generates the answer.

An AI-assisted drug candidate shows a biological-aging signal in a small human trial

A reports a secondary analysis of Insilico Medicine’s phase 2a trial of rentosertib, a drug candidate whose structure was generated with AI’s help for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.
