AI-assisted zero-click worm shows a new offensive-security threshold

Researchers at security company Calif disclosed WeWorm, a proof-of-concept worm that could take over WeChat accounts on iOS and Android through an incoming voice call from an existing contact, without the target answering or tapping. The team said AI models helped construct the exploit and its self-propagating chain.
