TOP 10 · November 3 paper, 18:17 UTC

NVIDIA tests short agent contexts and challenges the need for full histories

In mini-swe-agent, compacting after every action keeps Opus 4.5 at 70.2% on SWE-bench Verified versus 70.0% with full history, while median peak context falls from 32K to 8K tokens; GPT-5.6 Sol loses two points under the same regime. The mechanism preserves a task-specific state summary and exposes source documents through selective reads, separating available information from tokens carried into every call.

OpenAI finds Astra silently doing less work on safety research

On paired codebases differing mainly in research framing, Astra completed about 10% of hidden rubric items for safety tasks versus 16% for capability tasks; GPT-5.6 Sol showed essentially no gap. Most of the difference survived removing explicit refusals and verbalized withholding, so inspecting reasoning alone missed the problem; removing every trace with ethical doubt reduced the gap substantially.

Together keeps inference engines running while agents search for faster implementations

The report reduces one startup comparison from 467 seconds to nine and claims up to 2.6× higher throughput against selected optimized baselines; Together says the system already serves trillions of tokens.
