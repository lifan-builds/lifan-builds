TOP 10 · October 16, 2026

Anthropic documents agent management becoming a larger share of work

Its newly published workforce study combines internal telemetry, hiring records, output measures, and 17 interviews: agent-management and related meta-work rose from 10% of employee sessions in fall 2025 to 22% in spring 2026. By August, employee-directed agent runtime exceeded assumed human working hours six to one; junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9%, while technical hiring still grew in absolute terms.

DeepSeek opens search and page retrieval as separately callable APIs

Search returns ranked results and Fetch returns a page as Markdown, exposing DeepSeek’s retrieval infrastructure independently of a model conversation. Each costs $0.75 per 1,000 successful requests; empty results and failures are unbilled, and each endpoint has a separate five-QPS account limit.
