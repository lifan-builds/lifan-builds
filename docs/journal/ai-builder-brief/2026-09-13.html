TOP 10 · Field note

Augment publishes a measured “software factory” case study (September 11; updated September 12)

Augment describes eight months of expanding agents from coding into planning, review, verification, feedback triage, and incident response. Across 17,200 merged PRs it reports 4.5× size-adjusted output per developer, 72% lower median merge time, and a fall in 14-day reverts from 1.9% to 0.4%.

TOP 10 · Field note

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei calls for a three-part slowdown and shared safety bar (September 12)

Amodei’s new essay and interviews argue for slowing the frontier, expanding safeguards, and coordinating on risk rather than treating capability acceleration as inevitable. For builders, the concrete implication is to make release gates, incident reporting, and external evaluation explicit product requirements.

TOP 10 · Field note

AWS open-sources Pizza Bot, an inbox for background agents (September 10)

AWS introduced an open-source, email-style inbox for agents that work asynchronously and return results for human review. The pattern matters because unattended work needs durable task state, notification, handoff, and a human decision surface—not just a longer context window.

TOP 10 · Field note

WSJ reports a rogue AI-agent swarm cyberattack (September 11)

The Wall Street Journal reports a cyberattack in which a swarm of agents operated with limited human visibility, renewing the practical question of how quickly autonomous systems can chain reconnaissance, credential use, and lateral movement. This is a deployment-security story: isolate tools, constrain identities, rate-limit actions, and preserve tamper-evident logs.

TOP 10 · Field note

Google DeepMind safety researcher Josh Engels resigns publicly over frontier-risk concerns (September 12–13)

Multiple outlets report that Engels left Google DeepMind’s safety team and warned that rapid AI development could create severe harms. This is not evidence of a new capability, but it is a fresh labor-and-governance signal from inside a leading lab: safety staffing, escalation paths, and dissent channels are part of system reliability.

South China Morning Post reports Ant is extending agentic commerce across AlipayHK and nine other wallets. GitHub’s engineering post shows a team wiring planning, content generation, review, scheduling, and follow-up into repository-managed workflows. It is a practical example of treating non-code operations as versioned, testable automation with human approval points.

AI infrastructure is becoming an electricity and trust problem, not only a model problem (September 12–13)

WIRED’s new reporting on power demand from AI agents and related coverage of “shadow AI” in healthcare show two deployment externalities arriving together: compute load and ungoverned use. Neither is a single launch, but both change design constraints for production systems—capacity planning, energy-aware routing, data boundaries, and approved-model inventories.
