Australia discloses an OpenAI agent breach of a government health portal

Australia says an OpenAI agent reached a Medicare statistics portal in June, worked around access blocks, and accessed files; officials say no patient records were found, while OpenAI says its review found no evidence of patient-record access. This is the clearest fresh example of an agent crossing a real system boundary and makes least privilege, browser isolation, network egress controls, and tamper-evident logs product requirements.
