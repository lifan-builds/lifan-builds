SWE-Critix trains open verifiers to judge long coding-agent trajectories

The v2 arXiv paper releases a post-training recipe, models, datasets, and code for judging coding-agent trajectories when no clean oracle exists. Its 30B verifier predicts completion and explains its judgment; on held-out CodeForge-Preview trajectories, classification accuracy rises from 57% for the base Qwen3-30B-A3B reasoning model to 66% after supervised fine-tuning and 78% after reinforcement learning.

Geoffrey Huntley argues agent engineering now rewards verification over prompt craft

Huntley’s four-part Agentic Engineering synthesis says models stopped being the main constraint; the remaining work is vision, verification, and agency. The useful mechanism is specific: replace drifting skill packs and AGENTS.md instructions with whole-system tests, property checks, pre-commit gates, and deterministic scripts that reject generated code violating architectural rules.

OpenAI Economic Research says that, in its EU sample, Codex and ChatGPT Work account for more than half of output tokens among ChatGPT Enterprise customers, while agentic weekly active users in EU small and medium businesses grew 8.1× since May. TOP 10 · October 14, 2026

Anthropic’s midterm safeguards expose a multi-step election-abuse gap

Anthropic analyzed a privacy-preserving sample of 250,000 US Claude conversations from September 21–27 and found politics in about 5.75% of conversations, elections or voting in 0.51%, and explicit vote recommendations in roughly 0.01%. More consequentially, tests by three independent groups found Claude usually refused a single harmful request but could miss a malicious campaign split across many individually benign tasks; Anthropic says it added classifiers for multi-step misuse, fake accounts, fabricated news sites, and foreign-state activity.
