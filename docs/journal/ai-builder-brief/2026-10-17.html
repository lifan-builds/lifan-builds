TOP 10 · October 15, 2026

Claude Code’s team cuts review loops while reducing reverted changes

Anthropic’s engineering retrospective reports that merged PRs more than doubled from June to September while the reverted share fell over 40%. Its useful mechanism is convergence: cap review after three rounds, narrow subsequent passes to changed code, suppress repeated findings, and turn incidents into daily reviewer evaluations; mean reviews-to-pass fell from 2.08 to 1.32.

TOP 10 · October 15, 2026

Goodfire argues native neurons deserve another look before decomposition

Nick Cammarata’s extensive essay advances a working hypothesis, with the language-model companion paper still forthcoming: apparent polysemanticity often reflects related generalization and imperfect inspection tools. He estimates roughly 90% of Qwen3-32B neuron poles have one main function, explicitly dependent on thresholds and definitions; his reanalysis treats the famous cat/car neuron as a shared V-shape detector.

TOP 10 · October 15, 2026

Alex Wang derives transformer layers from memory interference and recall

This new essay develops linear layers, MLPs, and attention as memory stores, with runnable code and simulations rather than a new model announcement. Under random-key/value assumptions, an outer-product store reaches interference comparable to its signal near the key dimension; least-squares storage roughly doubles that capacity, while nonlinear recall suppresses irrelevant memories more sharply.

Anthropic’s evaluator-guided agents discover cheaper quantum circuit designs

Researchers report improved designs across eight quantum primitives: seven reduce both Toffoli gates and qubits, while reversible modular inversion improves their tradeoff. A disclosed point-addition design uses 17 times fewer gates with 12% more qubits than the smallest previous design; a separate decoder cuts gate cost twelvefold.

China proposes AI export controls and starts interim registration

The commerce and science ministries opened consultation through November 15 on 40 technology categories, including 23 covering AI models, agents, computing clusters, and information processing. Separately from the proposed final controls, the notice requires pre-export registration for listed technologies during the interim, including covered exports already underway.

Ultramarine’s report details reinforcement learning for non-verifiable professional work

Paper Instruments’ new model and technical report describe post-training GLM-5.3 Flash for finance, law, consulting, and operations, with a reported 4.1-point average gain across six knowledge-work benchmarks and 6.5% fewer output tokens. More useful than its marketing comparison is the task/rubric co-design: specify substantive success conditions without overfitting one reference answer, separate model errors from infrastructure failures, and keep graders outside agent control.

Claude reconstructs ECDSA seed origins with publicly reproducible arithmetic

Anthropic reports that an internal research model traced major NIST elliptic-curve seeds to the C library’s lrand48 generator initialized with 1997–98 timestamps, providing code that regenerates their published values. It also reconstructs six early base points through a different historical generator, while B-163’s seed and several other base points remain unexplained.
