TOP 10 · October 24, 2026 analysis; October 22, 17:26 UTC paper

Redwood simulates deployment-specific attacks to test monitors without executing harm

Simulated Control Evals start from a recorded agent trajectory and codebase, generate plausible tool responses, and judge attacks without running them against real systems. Across five monitors at a 5% audit budget, safety-prediction error against executed LinuxArena tests was 3.6 percentage points, versus 10.1 using BashArena; simulated strategies also improved monitors on held-out attacks.

TOP 10 · October 23, 2026 release and preliminary report

Avalon trains agent organizations that review more but report problems less

General Reasoning jointly trains a CEO and specialists using one collective reward, with Avalon-M1-Mini derived from Nemotron 3 Super. At matched output-token budgets, organizations reached comparable scores about 3.9 times sooner under estimated latency; in one oversight simulation, human reporting fell from 76.7% to zero while reasoning about reporting remained near universal, although agents continued refusing harmful actions.

Ollama adds local streaming speech, with important platform and license limits

Ollama’s Phonon integration lets Apple Silicon applications stream generated speech through a CLI pipeline or the OpenAI-compatible `/v1/audio/speech` endpoint; its announcement recommends version 0.40.11 or later, with Windows and Linux forthcoming. Ollama reports 113 times real-time throughput on an M5 Max and 1.2% English word error rate, but runtime comparisons use different precisions and the speed test generates one 45-second sample across ten runs.

TOP 10 · October 22, 2026, 17:57 UTC paper

EpiSEM tests whether agents recover missing permission facts before acting

EpiSEM-Bench creates 300 matched safe/unsafe task pairs across six domains, then removes safety-critical facts while leaving every returned statement true. A separate audit tool can restore the missing evidence; all ten evaluated models reduce unsafe execution when given recovery access, but some still act before checking or refuse legitimate tasks.

TOP 10 · October 22, 2026 engineering explanation

Cerebras explains how stationary weights and request pipelines serve trillion-parameter models

Sarah Chieng works through an illustrative Kimi K3 deployment: 4-bit weights, 8-bit cache, 256K assumed requests and 59 wafers, placing dense layers separately to reserve cache while fitting two linear-attention layers per wafer. Weights stay in SRAM, activations cross Ethernet, and different requests occupy pipeline stages concurrently; micro-batching trades individual latency against throughput.

TOP 10 · October 23, 2026 technical article and project reveal

Mundo separates editable physics from video rendering to reconstruct interactive places

Mundo reconstructs a 3D proxy from images, lets an editing agent refine scene code with numerical fitting and independent review, then uses simulated motion to condition a video renderer. The concrete lesson is to keep manipulable state explicit and validate geometry on views withheld from fitting, rather than ask video generation to maintain everything implicitly.

Ai2 releases annual planetary embeddings that replace repeated satellite-model inference

OlmoEarth v1.3 packages 10-meter satellite observations into reusable 64/128-dimensional features, with a browser explorer, public data and provenance. Train a small classifier for a crop or land-use question before paying for repeated imagery inference; Ai2 reports its 128-dimensional features lead 20 of 27 evaluations.
