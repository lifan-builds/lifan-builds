In a new Latent Space interview, Google researcher John Platt explains Empirical Research Assistance (ERA): define a scoreable scientific task, keep a tree of notebook experiments, use an upper-confidence rule to select promising branches, and have Gemini mutate multiple candidates while sharing branch history.
