OpenAI says a 10,000-agent system solved Navier–Stokes

TOP 10 · Original publication: September 8, 2026

New analysis says better data drove three times more pretraining efficiency than model recipes

Dwarkesh Patel and Jerry Han compared pretraining recipes and datasets from 2019–2025 and estimate that, at a 10^19-FLOP budget, data improvements yielded a 12.0-fold compute-efficiency gain versus 3.7-fold from model improvements—about 3.24 times more contribution from data. Why it matters: If the decomposition generalizes, teams underinvesting in filtering, deduplication, curricula, and data mixtures are optimizing the wrong layer.
