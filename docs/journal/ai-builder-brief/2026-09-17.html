HarnessTax finds similar coding-agent accuracy at radically different cost (September 16)

UC Berkeley and Arena researchers evaluated 21 model–harness pairs—seven models across Claude Code, Codex CLI, and Pi—on 30 sampled SWE-bench Lite and 30 Terminal-Bench 2.0 tasks, with three attempts per task. Harness choice changed success rates little on these benchmarks but changed cost by as much as 5×.

Z.ai says an infra agent recursively optimized GLM-5.3-Flash into full production (September 17)

Z.ai founder Jie Tang says its Infra Agent moved GLM-5.3-Flash to all production traffic on domestic accelerators in under two weeks and raised throughput 3.2× through repeated profiling, kernel changes, deployment, and live feedback. The concrete mechanism—not the “self-improvement” label—is what matters: a layered agent proposes low-level optimizations, verifies them against serving telemetry, and iterates inside a bounded production loop.
