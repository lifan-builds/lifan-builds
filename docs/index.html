About

What I build on nights and weekends, and why most of it stays local.

Say hello on X (@LifanBuilds) ↗

I’m a Staff Engineer at Google in Seattle. Outside work, I build local Mac utilities, multi-agent harnesses, and snow or gear trackers whenever existing apps get bloated, overpriced, or start cramming ads onto my screens.

My defaults are simple: keep private data on your own machine, verify speed and token wins with real runs instead of slides, and stop for human approval before anything touches an external account.

Go Python TypeScript Swift / macOS AX Chrome MV3 / CDP Gemini / Antigravity Codex
